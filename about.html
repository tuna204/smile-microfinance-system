Who We Are

We're the Smile.

We go beyond the norm, offering services based on ethical standards. Smile Multipurpose Cooperative Society Limited is a legally registered cooperative organization established to empower people through savings, affordable financing, investments, and sustainable development initiatives.

Through our flagship initiative, Smile World, we are building one of Africa's most comprehensive multipurpose cooperative ecosystems — delivering financial solutions alongside opportunities in finance, agriculture, housing, health, travel & transportation, technology and education.

Our adventure began with one aim: putting smiles on faces. From that moment, we knew we were destined to impact countless lives around the world.
