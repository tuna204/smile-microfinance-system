Who We Are

Welcome to Smile.

Smile Multipurpose Cooperative Society Limited is a legally registered cooperative built to empower people through savings, affordable financing, investments, and sustainable development.

Through our flagship initiative, Smile World, we're building one of Africa's most comprehensive cooperative ecosystems - spanning finance, agriculture, housing, health, travel, technology and education.

  • Ethics, Empathy, Excellence & Innovation
  • Regulated, secure member data handling
  • Loans available after 6 months of consistent saving
